Who reports what, and how

Sorted by reporting coverage. Itemised loggers file real tasks; bulk loggers book most of a week to one line, so their hours describe a calendar, not a workload. The strip shows which weeks each person filed. Coverage counts from the start of the export, except for the two people whose tenure is recorded as zero and who first appear in late August — they have filed every week since, and a flat denominator showed them as the worst reporters on the team. Appearing late is not on its own evidence of joining late: most people who first appear in week two simply missed week one, and several have four to six years of tenure.

What the loader did to the files

Which fiscal calendar the quarters use

The CRM extract gives a fiscal period but never a close date, so the year-start has to be inferred — and the inference is weak. Candidates are ranked first on work never landing after the quarter a deal closed in, an impossible case for pre-sale work. That test eliminates exactly one candidate: January, at 94%. The other eleven all score 100%, so the ranking then falls to same-quarter frequency, where February leads on 62% against March's 19%. The awkward part: January, the one eliminated, is also the one that would win on same-quarter frequency alone, at 75%. On sixteen matched deals this does not settle anything. February is used because it clears the first test and leads the remaining eleven on the second — not because the others failed anything: March also scores 100% and simply ranks lower on same-quarter frequency. Change FISCAL_START_MONTH in pipeline.py if the real calendar is known.
