What the loader did to the files

Which fiscal calendar the quarters use

The CRM extract gives a fiscal period but never a close date, so the year-start has to be inferred. For each opportunity the pipeline could match, work should land in or before the quarter the deal closes in. Every candidate year-start was scored on that. The winner is used throughout; change FISCAL_START_MONTH in pipeline.py if the real calendar differs.
